# Proof Sprint: Vancouver Date Night

## Verdict

Run the first CityAtlas proof sprint around Vancouver date night.

This wedge is the best first business-building lane because it is naturally social, visual, recurring, and easy to package as a route instead of a flat directory page. A couple does not want "a restaurant database." They want a confident plan. A business does not want "another listing." It wants qualified local intent, context, and repeatable placement inside a guide or mission.

## Safety State

- Status: research packet ready.
- Public state: do not publish real business pages yet.
- Outreach state: do not send.
- Payment state: no Stripe, checkout, invoice, deposit, or payment link.
- Source state: public source links gathered for candidate review only.
- Data state: candidates are seeded into the private owner console as review-only proof candidates, not public listings.
- CRM state: candidates include official-source contact-path confidence and remain visible in the private owner console only.

## First Mission Draft

Name: Two-Hour Date Night Loop

Audience: couples, visitors, and locals who want one confident evening plan.

Mission shape:

1. Pick the mood: intimate, waterfront, cocktail-forward, dessert-forward, or culture-first.
2. Choose one anchor: dinner or experience.
3. Choose one closer: dessert, bar, waterfront walk, or gallery/event.
4. Save the route.
5. Share a simple invite draft.

Founder demo version:

- Start with a private source-backed mock route.
- Show the fictional CityAtlas mission UI first.
- Then show how one real candidate could be verified and reviewed.
- End with the partner value: "You are not buying a listing. You are buying placement inside a plan."

## 7-Day Proof Sprint

Day 1: finalize candidate list and source policy.

Day 2: build one private date-night mission brief using only sourced, paraphrased facts.

Day 3: choose prospects from the shadow ranking and review partner fit, route fit, contact confidence, and founder access.

Day 4: owner reviews outreach drafts and approves a narrow manual outreach batch.

Day 5: send only after approval, manually, with no automation.

Day 6: log replies, objections, demo requests, and warm-intro leads, then run `npm run replies:analyze`.

Day 7: decide whether to continue date night, shift to wellness, or run a parallel activity wedge.

## Evidence Ladder

1. Local proof: app works, route concept works, admin proof sprint is visible.
2. Source proof: every real candidate has an official source link and review checklist.
3. Conversation proof: prospects reply or ask to see the preview.
4. Collaboration proof: one or more businesses approve a private or public feature.
5. Revenue proof: a business agrees to a founding package after terms and payment approval.

## Metrics

- Primary metric: meaningful replies or demo requests from 10 to 20 owner-reviewed prospects.
- Secondary metric: which route angle creates the fastest positive response.
- Guardrail metric: any confusion that CityAtlas is already live, already ranking pages, or already accepting payments.

## Do Not Say Yet

- "Featured on CityAtlas" unless the business has approved the context.
- "Best in Vancouver" unless using a sourced, current, attributed, and legally reviewed claim.
- "We can drive bookings" before real analytics exist.
- "We have traffic" before analytics exist.
- "Paid partner" before payment terms are approved.
