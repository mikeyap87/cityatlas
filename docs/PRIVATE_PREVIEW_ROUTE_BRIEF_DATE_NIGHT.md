# Private Preview Route Brief: Vancouver Date Night

## Purpose

This brief defines what CityAtlas can show in a private founder preview before public launch.

The goal is to make the product feel real to a business owner without implying that any real business is listed, partnered, endorsed, ranked, or approved.

Local route: `/private-preview/date-night`

## Preview Rules

Allowed:

- show the fictional CityAtlas app first
- explain the date-night route concept
- show a source-backed candidate queue in the owner console
- show one private route concept using candidate names as review examples
- say that public use requires source review and approval

Not allowed:

- publish the preview publicly
- send a public URL that can be indexed
- imply the business is already featured
- imply any partnership or endorsement
- use copied images, menu text, reviews, ratings, or awards
- claim traffic, booking volume, rankings, or revenue

## Demo Sequence

1. Open `/`.
   - Message: CityAtlas is a Vancouver-first local discovery product.

2. Open `/vancouver/missions`.
   - Message: missions package discovery into a plan people can save and share.

3. Open `/planner`.
   - Message: the product creates intent signals before payment or public claims.

4. Open `/for-businesses/pricing`.
   - Message: CityAtlas keeps the review-first path clear and now also has a limited hosted-checkout option for paid partner packages when the fit is already obvious.

5. Open `/admin`.
   - Message: the owner console keeps real-world actions gated.

6. Scroll to Founder CRM.
   - Message: the first candidate queue is review-only and not public.

7. Open `/private-preview/date-night`.
   - Message: this is the screen-share-safe route story for a private founder conversation.

## Route Concepts

These are private-review concepts, not public recommendations.

### Main Street Tasting Route

Anchor candidate: Published on Main.

Possible shape:

- dinner anchor
- dessert closer
- save/share prompt

Use when the business understands premium local dining and route placement quickly.

### Chinatown Intimate Route

Anchor candidate: Kissa Tanto.

Possible shape:

- intimate dinner
- cocktail closer
- short walk or nearby cultural stop

Use when the prospect fit is strong but claim language must stay especially careful.

### Gastown Candlelit Route

Anchor candidate: L'Abattoir.

Possible shape:

- dinner or bar anchor
- cocktail closer
- route share prompt

Use when demonstrating that CityAtlas can create neighborhood-level route context.

### Waterfront Celebration Route

Anchor candidate: Miku Waterfront.

Possible shape:

- waterfront dinner
- dessert or walk closer
- special-occasion framing

Use when showing a visitor-friendly or celebration route.

### Culture-First Date Route

Anchor candidate: Vancouver Art Gallery.

Possible shape:

- gallery or exhibition starter
- dinner nearby
- dessert or cocktail closer

Use when showing that CityAtlas can go beyond restaurants and build a whole evening.

## Private Preview CTA

Use:

"Would this kind of route placement be useful enough for your team to review further?"

Do not use:

"Do you want to become a paid partner?"

## Review Checklist Before Any Preview Link Leaves Local Mode

- recipient is approved
- route concept is approved
- source URLs are saved
- no images are copied
- no rankings or awards are used
- no "best" claim appears
- no payment link appears
- correction/removal language is included
- preview cannot be indexed

## What To Log After A Preview

- who saw it
- what route concept they saw
- whether they understood the offer
- whether they asked for pricing
- whether they raised a data/source concern
- whether they asked to be included, corrected, or removed
- next step

Use the admin Reply Tracker after manual owner-approved outreach. The tracker is local-only and does not send or sync messages.

For reply-specific manual language, use `docs/proof-sprints/DATE_NIGHT_REPLY_RESPONSE_KIT.md`. That kit does not approve a hosted preview link, follow-up, payment ask, or second outreach batch.
