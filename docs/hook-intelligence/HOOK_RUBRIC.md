# CityAtlas Hook Rubric

Use this to score guide titles, article openings, landing-page openings, and short city-content hooks before the draft moves forward.

## 100-Point Rubric

| Category | Weight | What it tests |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| City Intent Clarity | 15 | Does the opening make the Vancouver situation clear fast? |
| Audience Fit | 10 | Does it call in the right local, visitor, couple, or business viewer? |
| Specific Usefulness | 15 | Is there a real route, decision, or planning payoff? |
| Non-Generic Angle | 15 | Does it avoid sounding like generic travel SEO sludge? |
| Route Logic | 10 | Does it imply a simple next step or city-planning structure? |
| Trust Honesty | 15 | Can CityAtlas support the claim without fake "best of" language? |
| Brand Memory | 10 | Does it feel like CityAtlas, not any city blog? |
| Hook-To-Body Fit | 5 | Does the rest of the draft naturally fulfill the promise? |
| Hook-To-CTA Fit | 5 | Does it lead naturally to a guide, mission, planner, or business step? |

Total: 100

## Minimum Pass Rules

- Total score must be at least 84
- `City Intent Clarity` must be at least 11/15
- `Specific Usefulness` must be at least 11/15
- `Trust Honesty` must be at least 12/15
- no hard fails

## Hard Fails

- could describe almost any city, not Vancouver
- sounds like a generic listicle opener
- promises verified recommendations when the draft is still demo-only
- uses fake urgency or fake scarcity
- says "best" or "top" without reviewed proof
- attracts the wrong audience for the CTA

## Strong Hook Families

- one confident plan for a specific city moment
- how to choose the right neighborhood for a mood or outcome
- how to avoid a common city-planning mistake
- compact route logic for a real scenario
- better-than-generic-map decision help

## Weak Hook Families

- endless things-to-do lists
- vague "discover Vancouver like never before" copy
- generic travel hype
- exaggerated insider language without proof
- clickbait built around fake local authority
