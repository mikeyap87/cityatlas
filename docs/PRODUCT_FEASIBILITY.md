# Product Feasibility Notes

## Verdict

Build a smaller MVP first: local/private preview, manual verified data, founder-led sales, and review-gated partner pages.

Do not build full self-serve public marketplace, automated data import, automated outreach, or live subscriptions until the core sales motion is validated.

## Required Services Later

- Hosting: Vercel or equivalent static app hosting.
- Database/auth: Supabase or similar.
- Payments: Stripe after terms/refund/pricing approval.
- Email/newsletter: provider selected after privacy/compliance review.
- Business data: official APIs or manually verified business submissions.
- Analytics: privacy-approved event tracking.

## Safest MVP

- private preview on approved domain/subdomain
- 10 to 20 manually verified business pages
- guide/category pages for one vertical
- manual founding partner outreach
- local/CRM tracking of replies and objections
- no checkout until 2 to 3 businesses validate the package

## Cut From V1

- automated scraping
- public user accounts
- reviews/comments
- self-serve checkout
- AI-generated public claims
- multi-city expansion
- automated outreach campaigns
