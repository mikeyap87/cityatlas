## Design System

- White public-product surface.
- Deep ink typography.
- Blue/green civic discovery accents.
- Compact 6 to 8px cards and controls.
- Map-like discovery panel.
- Visible payment and review gates.
- Product-first homepage rather than generic marketing hero.

## Fidelity Notes

The implementation follows the concept structure:

- public header and search
- Vancouver hero image
- map/discovery panel
- explore cards
- featured business detail preview
- founding partner packages
- payment disabled notice
- owner dashboard preview
- mobile-safe responsive layout

The implementation intentionally keeps text code-native and does not ship the concept screenshot as UI.
