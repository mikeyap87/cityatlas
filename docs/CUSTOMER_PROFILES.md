# CityAtlas Customer Profiles

## 1. Local Explorer

### Who they are

A local resident who wants a better plan than generic search results or listicles.

### What they want

- trustworthy route ideas
- useful city planning help
- clear choices without research overload

### Why CityAtlas fits

CityAtlas is strongest when it feels like an answer engine for real city intent, not a directory.

## 2. Out-Of-Town Visitor

### Who they are

A visitor who wants a cleaner local route, date plan, or decision path than broad travel-search clutter.

### What they want

- one useful route
- local confidence
- fewer weak options

### Why CityAtlas fits

The product can narrow city decisions into a smaller, more credible path.

## 3. Local Business Or Founder

### Who they are

A service business, venue, or founder who wants better-fit discovery and future lead flow.

### What they want

- visibility in the right local context
- better-fit intent
- higher-quality discovery than commodity listings

### Why CityAtlas fits

The business side only works if the public route layer first becomes genuinely useful.

## 4. City Connector

### Who they are

Someone with local taste, network reach, or partner leverage who can amplify strong city routes.

### What they want

- a credible local surface
- a product that respects city quality
- trust before scale

### Why CityAtlas fits

CityAtlas becomes more powerful when trusted local routes can be shared through real connectors instead of only search traffic.
