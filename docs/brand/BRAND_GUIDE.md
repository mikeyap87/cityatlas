# CityAtlas Brand Guide

CityAtlas is a Univenture Studio project for city discovery and local-business growth. The brand should feel useful, civic, confident, and route-first: a product people use to decide where to go, not a decorative travel magazine.

## Brand Idea

CityAtlas turns a city into practical routes, trusted local context, and business visibility.

## Logo System

Primary mark: a map pin containing a folded atlas and route line.

Use it for:

- app header icon
- favicon
- social/profile avatar
- launch decks and proof-sprint documents
- partner packet cover pages

Current assets:

- `public/brand/cityatlas-mark.svg`
- `public/brand/cityatlas-lockup.svg`
- `public/favicon.svg`

Do not replace the mark with a generic map, compass, globe, skyline, or location-pin icon. If a page needs the CityAtlas brand, use `CityAtlasMarkIcon` from `src/components/Icons.tsx` or the SVG asset above.

## Color Roles

- Ink: `#0B1726` for trust, headers, and high-contrast UI.
- Atlas gold: `#F3B53C` for the brand mark and key moments.
- Route green: `#0E8B5A` for primary actions, route logic, and progress.
- Water blue: `#0A5EA8` for discovery links, metadata, and city context.
- Soft surface: `#F6F9FB` and `#EEF7FB` for panels and low-pressure review spaces.

Avoid turning the whole product into a single green, blue, amber, or dark-slate theme. CityAtlas should feel structured and civic, with color used as wayfinding.

## Product Voice

Use plain, specific, operational language:

- "Plan a date-night route"
- "Review this business"
- "Log a reply"
- "Payment acceptance is disabled"
- "Source review required"

Avoid hype claims:

- "best in Vancouver"
- "guaranteed bookings"
- "partnered with"
- "featured by"
- "official guide"

## Public Trust Rules

- Fictional seed content must stay clearly framed as demo content.
- Real business names in proof-sprint surfaces are review examples until source, privacy, and publication gates are approved.
- Do not publish rankings, endorsements, copied descriptions, menu text, review excerpts, or traffic claims without approval.
