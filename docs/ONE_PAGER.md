# CityAtlas One-Pager

## What It Is

CityAtlas is a Vancouver-first city discovery and local-business growth platform.

It is built around answer-first route pages, useful local guides, and business-side growth paths rather than a generic directory.

## Who It Serves

- Locals who want a stronger city plan.
- Visitors who want a higher-quality route or recommendation path.
- Service businesses, venues, and founders who want better local discovery and lead quality.
- City connectors or partners who can help distribute trusted local routes.

## Problem

Most city discovery surfaces are shallow, outdated, or built like SEO directories rather than useful city guidance.

CityAtlas tries to win by combining source-backed city guidance with future business-growth surfaces.

## Core Offer

- Public city route and guide surfaces
- Vancouver-first local discovery experience
- Search-friendly answer pages
- Future lead and partner growth paths for local businesses
