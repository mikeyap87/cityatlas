## Warm Intro Ask

Hey `{contact_name}` - I am building CityAtlas, a Vancouver local discovery product around curated routes. The first proof sprint is date night. Do you know anyone at `{business_name}` who handles partnerships, media, or guest experience?

## Do Not Include

- Payment links.
- "Featured partner" language before approval.
- "Best in Vancouver" claims.
- Traffic or booking promises.
- Automated follow-up language.
- Any claim that a business has joined CityAtlas.

## Manual Sending Gate

Before any send, owner must approve:

- final recipients
- exact copy
- channel
- sender account
- send count
- reply logging location
- opt-out handling
