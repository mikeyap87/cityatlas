# CityAtlas Preliminary Name Preflight

Date: 2026-06-14

## Candidate

- Name: CityAtlas
- Project: CityAtlas
- Category: Local discovery, city guide, business visibility SaaS
- Intended public use: Public product name under Univenture Studio, starting at `city.univenturestudio.com`

## Product-Fit Check

- What the product actually is: a Vancouver-first local discovery and local-business growth platform.
- What the name signals well: city coverage, mapping, local guidance, organized discovery.
- What the name may incorrectly imply: official municipal data, GIS/mapping authority, or a generic city atlas rather than a curated growth product.

## Web Knock-Out Check

- Exact-name results found: no obvious exact public product collision found in the quick public web search performed during this pass.
- Related-category collisions found: related local discovery and city-guide category players include Foursquare City Guide, Citymapper, Google Local Guides, Spotted by Locals, and GuidePal.
- Notes: exact `CityAtlas` remains only preliminary from this pass. The words `city` and `atlas` are descriptive/generic enough that distinctiveness is not strong.

## Trademark Knock-Out Check

- USPTO or equivalent search checked: USPTO trademark search page was checked as the official starting point, but the actual search application requires JavaScript and was not fully queried in this environment.
- Exact or similar mark found: no exact official-record result was confirmed in this pass.
- Related goods/services: city guides, mapping, local search, travel, discovery, business promotion.
- Notes: this is not legal clearance. Counsel or a complete trademark-record search should happen before public brand lock, domain purchase, or paid campaign investment.

## Domain / Operating-Surface Check

- `.com`: not confirmed in this pass.
- `.app`: not confirmed in this pass.
- likely operating domain: `city.univenturestudio.com`
- Notes: use the Univenture subdomain first to avoid over-committing before name clearance.

## Distinctiveness Notes

- crowded terms used: `city`, `atlas`
- meaning/phonetic overlap: many adjacent products use city, local, guide, map, atlas, route, and discovery language.
- overall distinctiveness: moderate-low; usable as an internal/project codename and provisional public name, but not final.

## Preliminary Decision

- Label: `provisional`
- Internal-use answer: yes, safe enough for internal Univenture project management.
- Public-use answer: safe enough for protected preview only; not enough for final trademark/domain commitment.

## Evidence

- USPTO trademark search starting point: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search
- Foursquare City Guide related-category reference: https://foursquare.com/city-guide
- Citymapper related-category reference: https://citymapper.com/
- Google Local Guides related-category reference: https://maps.google.com/localguides
- Spotted by Locals related-category reference: https://www.spottedbylocals.com/

## Next Move

- Keep CityAtlas provisional.
- Use `city.univenturestudio.com` for protected preview if approved.
- Complete a deeper trademark/domain search before public launch, standalone domain purchase, or final brand assets.
